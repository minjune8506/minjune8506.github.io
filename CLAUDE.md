# CLAUDE.md

Jekyll-based personal tech blog (GitHub Pages). Posts live in `_posts/`, written in Korean.

## Writing style for post content

One idea per paragraph. Do not pack multiple sentences into a single dense paragraph — split at natural sentence/topic boundaries into separate paragraphs (blank line between them), roughly 1-2 sentences each.

This applies to body paragraphs and list items alike: a bullet or paragraph that chains 3+ sentences of reasoning together should be broken up, not left as one block.

Two closely related sentences (cause → immediate effect) can stay together if splitting would feel choppy. When in doubt, split — the user has repeatedly asked for shorter, less dense paragraphs when reviewing drafts.
